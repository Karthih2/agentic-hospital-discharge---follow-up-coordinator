# Landing page surface brief

Mode: Persuade (landing). The app screens under /app are Operate and follow desgin/frontend.md.

Audience, job, action: a patient, family member, judge or hospital reviewer decides in seconds what this is and tries the demo or signs in. Real content only: the synthetic sample summary, the plan the real backend produced for it, six real translations of one task. No testimonials, customers, pricing or metrics beyond product facts (7 item types, 6 languages, 2 AI agents of 9).

Constraints (binding, from the user): the icon sheet's sliced icons only, no lucide, no gradients, no shadows, no pure white, no emoji, no em dashes, no hover animation, no animated arrows, no bento or rows of feature cards, no kickers, small corner radius, user's teal palette (#0E7C7B, #5ED6C3, #EAFBF6) joined to the sheet's navy ink and status colours, skeleton loaders, Terms and Privacy pages, a real product demo.

User chose "traditional landing format". Clean and structured, not overbearing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, traditional landing page where the product itself is the proof: a synthetic discharge sheet becomes a plan with a locked Needs Review item. It refuses the SaaS default of three icon cards, gradient hero and floating orbs.
OWN-WORLD: Flat mint ground (#EAFBF6) with deeper mint bands, deep teal committed sections, navy ink (#14304F) text and 2px outlines like the icon sheet. Icons sit on flat sticker tiles with 2px navy borders and 4px corners. Status squares: blue Pending, green Completed, orange Needs Review. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body, Noto Sans for the six scripts.
STORY: The visitor understands that discharge papers become a dated plan, that fixed rules and a doctor handle anything unclear, that it works in six languages, and that the patient controls family access. They try the demo or sign in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a large headline "Your discharge papers, turned into a plan you can follow." with one supporting paragraph and two buttons (Try the demo primary, Sign in plain). Right, a bordered plan preview tile for a synthetic cardiac patient: a vertical timeline of six rows in status colours, a legend below. Header above with brand, five anchor links, language select, Sign in, Get started.
FORM: Traditional single column landing with alternating bands. Roughly: hero, seven kinds list, how it works vertical steps with a scroll-drawn line, ScrollReveal statement, teal safety band, live demo, languages marquee and six translations, roles with an access level toggle, FAQ, teal call to action, footer. Chosen by the user after a dealt round (form 7 of the grounded list was not taken). Seed key b35ef444.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
